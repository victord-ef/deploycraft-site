Threat intelligence is the difference between reacting to attacks after they succeed and making informed decisions about where to invest your defences before they are tested. Most organisations have more data than they can analyse and less context than they need — the result is alert fatigue, mis-prioritised patching, and a security posture built around yesterday's threats.

DeployCraft.io threat intelligence services are built around your specific threat profile: your industry, your technology stack, your geography, and your adversaries. We do not deliver generic feeds and vendor reports — we deliver intelligence that is directly relevant to your environment, your risk, and your team's capacity to act.

Intelligence is delivered in formats that integrate with your existing security tooling — STIX/TAXII for automated ingestion, structured reports for analyst workflows, and executive briefings for leadership decisions. Engagements are available as ongoing retained subscriptions or as targeted intelligence projects scoped to a specific adversary, campaign, or risk question.
