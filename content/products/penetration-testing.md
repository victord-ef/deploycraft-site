DeployCraft.io penetration testing is conducted manually by a practitioner with hands-on experience across the full SDLC — software engineering, quality operations, and cybersecurity. Every engagement goes beyond automated scanning: findings are validated, contextualised within your architecture, and delivered with actionable remediation guidance written for the engineers who will fix them.

Engagements are scoped to match your environment and risk appetite, with reporting structured for both technical teams and stakeholders.
